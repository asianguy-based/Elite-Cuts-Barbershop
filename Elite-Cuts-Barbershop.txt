Commercial Delivery Tip for Reselling
When you license this out to a barbershop owner, you don't even have to tell them where the hidden gear icon went. You simply hand them their custom manager link:

"Here is your client booking app: [https://eliteshop.com](https://eliteshop.com).
And here is your private manager portal bookmark: [https://eliteshop.com/?portal=admin]
(https://eliteshop.com/?portal=admin) (PIN: 1234)."

(https://api.qrserver.com/v1/create-qr-code/?size=130x130&data=YOUR_DEPLOYED_H5_URL))
