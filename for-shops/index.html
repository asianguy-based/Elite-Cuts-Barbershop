About your own web address

Your own domain is included free for the first year when it costs under $50 a year to register. After the first year, renewal is billed to the shop at the domain's price, agreed when we set everything up. Premium or resold names that cost $50 or more aren't included; you can pick a different name or ending, or pay the full cost.

Questions owners ask

Do my clients have to download an app?

No. They open your link or scan the QR card and add it to their home screen from the browser. It works on iPhone and Android.

I already use Square. Can I keep it?

Yes. Keep Square for payments. Your deposit link can be your own Square or Stripe payment link.

Who sets it up?

We do. Send us your services, prices, barbers, hours and a few cut photos, and we build it under your shop's name and colors.

I don't have a website. Does that matter?

That's a great fit. Your app shows your address, hours, tap-to-call, services and barbers, so it can be the Website link on your Google listing and in your Instagram bio.

What do clients need to book?

Their name and mobile number. The number also links their stamps and VIP points, so there's no password to remember.
